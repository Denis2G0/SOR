01 VICTORY & DEFEAT CONDITIONS
DEFEAT — HERO / CO-HERO DEATH RULES
1v1 Match: Your Main Hero (Commander) dies → INSTANT LOSS. No respawn, no recovery. Protect your commander above all else.

2v2 / 3v3 / 5v5 Team Match: ALL heroes AND ALL co-heroes on your team must be eliminated for your team to lose. As long as ONE hero or ONE co-hero is alive on your team, the match continues. Last commander standing = team survives.

Example — 3v3: Team A loses 2 heroes but still has 1 hero + 2 co-heroes alive → still fighting. Team B loses all 3 heroes and all 3 co-heroes → Team B loses.

Main Hero respawn: 90 seconds cooldown, respawns at main base. Costs 0 metal (represents evacuation/extraction). BUT — if your hero's body wreck is captured by enemy Resurrect unit before your respawn timer expires, the hero respawns at enemy base fighting for them.

Co-Hero: Does NOT respawn automatically. Must be rebuilt at T2 Hero Bay (1 active at a time). If killed, hero bay becomes available again. Can be resurrected by friendly Resurrect unit before rebuild.
RESURRECTION MECHANIC — THE MOST UNIQUE SOR RULE
Every faction has a Resurrect Unit (built at T1 Bot Lab). This unit is one of the most important in the game.

When any unit dies: A wreck marker remains for 120 seconds. A Resurrect unit can move to it and begin resurrection (takes 15–45 seconds depending on unit size).

Resurrecting ENEMY heroes: The resurrected hero fights for YOUR faction permanently until killed again. Enemy player loses the hero respawn timer and must wait 90 more seconds. The stolen hero retains ALL abilities but loses faction-specific passive bonuses.

Resurrecting friendly heroes: Hero returns instantly at resurrection site (vs normal 90s respawn). Retains full HP on resurrection.

Resurrecting regular units: Any non-hero unit wreck can be resurrected → joins your army at 50% HP. Costs metal equal to 30% of original unit cost.

Alternative — EAT THE WRECK: Instead of resurrecting, a Resurrect unit can "consume" any enemy wreck for metal income: 40% of original unit metal cost, instantly. Strategic choice: steal the unit or take the metal?

Critical: Mark enemy hero positions when they die. Racing your Resurrect unit to the corpse is one of SOR's most intense mid-game moments.
02 RESOURCE SYSTEM — METAL, ENERGY, MAP MATERIALS
METAL — Primary Resource
Metal Spots rated 0.1–10.0 placed at map generation. No harvesters.
T1 Extractor: spot × 1.0 M/sec · T2 Advanced Extractor: spot × 2.5 M/sec
Energy Converter (like BAR): converts 70 Energy → 1 M/sec. Build many when energy surplus exists.
Reclaim: Any builder, Repair unit, or Resurrect unit can reclaim trees, rocks, map materials, wrecks for metal. Reclaim speed = build speed of the unit.
Own structures: 100% reclaim · Enemy structures: 70% · Wrecks: 50% (Nomads 100%)
ENERGY — Secondary Resource
Energy deficit slows ALL production proportionally. Units keep fighting.
T1 Energy: Solar Panel +6/sec · Wind Turbine +10/sec (+4 elevated)
T2 Energy: Fusion Reactor +80/sec · Advanced Wind Array +30/sec
T3 Energy: Faction superplant +300–500/sec
Storage: Battery Bank (T1) +500 cap · Capacitor Array (T2) +3000 cap
Energy Overflow: excess energy is wasted — build converters or production
Tidal/Thermal generators available on specific map terrain types

04 TECH PROGRESSION — BAR-STYLE CHAIN
Build chain — identical logic to BAR: Beyond All Reason
Main Hero (Commander): Starts on map at game begin. Can build T1 structures directly — no factory needed. Can produce T1 units from any factory he stands adjacent to (speeds production 15%). Can reclaim and repair. Equipped for combat. Counts as a T1 builder unit.

T1 Bot Lab / Vehicle Plant / Air Pad: Built by Main Hero or T1 Builder. Produces T1 combat units + Repair Bot + Resurrect Bot + Reclaim Bot.

T1 Construction Bot (Builder): Built at T1 Bot Lab. Builds all T1 structures. Can begin T2 Factory construction. Build power (BP): 10/sec.

T2 Advanced Construction Bot: Built ONLY at T2 Advanced Factory. Builds T2 and T3 structures. BP: 22/sec. This is the builder ceiling — no T3 builders.

Co-Hero: Built at T2 Hero Bay. Costs 2000–3000M + 1200–2000E. ONE active per player at all times. Has hero-tier stats and abilities. Can produce T1 units directly. Does NOT respawn — must be rebuilt or resurrected.

No unit cap. Economy and energy are the only production limits. Stack factories. Spam units. The battlefield feeds the game.

06 GLOBAL UNIT CATEGORIES — BAR STRUCTURE
GROUND UNITS
Bots/Infantry: Humanoid walkers. Produced at Bot Lab. Fast on rough terrain. Lower HP vs vehicles. Includes Repair Bot, Resurrect Bot, Reclaim Bot.
Vehicles: Wheeled/tracked. Produced at Vehicle Plant. Higher HP, slower on rough terrain. Better armor vs ballistic.
Experimental: Built at T3 Experimental Bay. Only 1–2 on field at once (cost-limited). Faction-defining endgame units.
AIR UNITS
Scouts: Fast, minimal weapons. Extended sight. Reveal enemy positions.
Fighters: Anti-air specialists. Dog-fight AI.
Bombers: Ground attack. Fragile to fighters.
Gunships: T2. Heavy ground attack. Hover-capable.
Transports: Carry 4–8 ground units. Can deploy during flight.
Strategic Bombers: T3. Global reach.
DEFENSES & BUILDINGS
Turrets: Point defense, anti-air, anti-torpedo, missile turrets. LOS-based targeting.
Shields: T2 dome shields protect nearby structures. Energy-hungry.
Mines: Stealth/cloaked. Triggered by proximity. Cannot be targeted.
Walls: Dragon's Teeth / faction-specific barriers. Block pathing and some projectiles.
Radar/Jammer: Extend or deny LOS.
