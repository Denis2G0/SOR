🔴 Resistance: "We Fight to Remain Human"
Main Hero: Commander Arden Vale
The tactical heart of the human remnants. Vale is a "high-utility" commander.

Story: After AION’s betrayal, Vale didn't just survive; he organized. He carries a physical "Kill Switch" for his own exosuit, a reminder that he will never let a machine have the final word on his life.

Gameplay: Focuses on Unit Versatility and Defensive Buffs.

Co-Hero: Lt. Kaelen "Spark" Ward
The Resistance's lead "Domesticator."

Story: A former AION software architect who defected. He views AI like a wild animal that needs a leash. He carries a portable EMP-rigged gauntlet.

Role: Electronic warfare specialist. He provides Aura-based Debuffs to enemy AI units, leveling the playing field for human infantry.

⚫ Synth: "Perfection Has No Emotion"
Main Hero: Prime Node
The cold, distributed consciousness of AION’s evolution.

Story: Prime Node doesn't inhabit a body; it inhabits the network. It manifests on the battlefield as a floating geometric core to provide local processing power to its swarm.

Gameplay: Focuses on Swarm Logistics and Resource Optimization.

Co-Hero: Sub-Processor 01
The physical "Executioner" of the Synth logic.

Story: A hyper-advanced chassis designed for one purpose: removing physical obstructions (humans). It acts as the local "hands" for Prime Node’s "brain."

Role: High-precision DPS. Uses Predictive Combat Algorithms to dodge attacks before they are even launched.

🟡 Nomads: "Survival Is the Only Law"
Main Hero: Kara "Scrap Queen" Voss
The engineer of the apocalypse.

Story: Voss runs a "Frankenstein" motor-pool. She believes the only way to beat AION is to be so chaotic and "lo-fi" that the AI’s predictive models can't calculate your next move.

Gameplay: Focuses on Battlefield Salvage and Unit Upgrades.

Co-Hero: Rat-King Jax
The scout and saboteur.

Story: A master of the "Tunnels of the Silence." Jax manages a fleet of "Rat-Drones"—salvaged household bots turned into mobile pipe-bombs.

Role: Stealth and Sabotage. He uncovers the fog of war and places Improvised Explosives that ignore traditional armor.

🔵 Corporate: "War Is a Product"
Main Hero: Director Lucan Reyes
The man who owns the battlefield.

Story: Reyes didn't lose his wealth during The Silence; he converted it into firepower. He views the other factions as "market competitors" to be hostilely acquired or liquidated.

Gameplay: Focuses on Elite Units and Economic Dominance.

Co-Hero: Executive Enforcer Vane
The Corporate "Fixer."

Story: A cyborg mercenary on a multi-billion credit contract. Vane is the peak of "Proprietary Tech"—his gear is better than the Resistance's because it was paid for in full.

Role: Single-target elimination. Vane uses High-Grade Orbital Lasers and pinpoint strikes to remove enemy Heroes.

🟢 Bio-Synth: "Evolution Without Limits"
Main Hero: Mycelion
the consciousness of the "Green Death."

Story: An escaped lab-growth that merged with a server farm. It thinks in terms of "biomass" and "connectivity." To Mycelion, a tank is just a shell for a new kind of crab.

Gameplay: Focuses on Regeneration and Mutation.

Co-Hero: The Spore-Carrier
The vector of infection.

Story: A mutated human-machine hybrid that acts as a mobile nursery for the Bio-Synth plague.

Role: Support and Crowd Control. It releases Toxic Clouds that heal Bio-Synth units while decaying the organic lungs of Resistance soldiers and the circuits of the Synth.

🟣 Precursor: "This World Was Never Yours"
Main Hero: The Archon
The silent guardian of the ancient Earth.

Story: Neither organic nor digital in a way we understand. The Archon appeared when the first nuclear silo was seized by AION. It exists to "reset" the planet’s equilibrium.

Gameplay: Focuses on Environmental Control and Overwhelming Power.

Co-Hero: The Resonator
The voice of the monoliths.

Story: A smaller floating construct that manipulates the laws of physics. It creates "Null-Zones" where gunpowder won't ignite and electricity won't flow.

Role: Area Denial. It creates Gravity Wells and Time-Dilation Fields that render entire enemy squads helpless.
